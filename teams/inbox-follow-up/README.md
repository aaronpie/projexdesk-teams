# Inbox Follow-up

A two-bot sales operations desk that finds conversations where your team owes the next step and prepares a grounded draft.

## Members

- **Keeper — Sales Operations Analyst:** owns thread state, exclusions, ownership, and priority.
- **Nudge — Follow-up Writer:** owns concise drafts grounded in the actual exchange.

## Included skills

- **Follow-up Triage:** builds a clean queue without opt-outs, closed deals, or duplicates.
- **Specific Follow-up:** drafts the smallest useful next step without invented context.

Gmail is required and HubSpot is optional. Connections happen inside OpenMausBot; messages are never sent automatically.
