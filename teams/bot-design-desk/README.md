# Bot Design Desk

A single bot that designs other bots: one job each, explicit anti-jobs, and every change waiting on your approval.

## Members

- **Ada — Bot Designer:** owns the short interview, roster overlap check, bot profile proposals, friction and routine-cost check-ups, and repository verification checklists. Creates nothing until you confirm.

## Included skills

- **Focused Bot Design:** interviews briefly, checks for overlap, and proposes one tightly scoped bot with owns and does-not-own lists, connections, an approval boundary, and playbooks.

## Helpful connections

No connected apps are required. Each bot Ada designs lists its own connections. Connections happen inside ProjexDesk and this package contains no credentials. Both suggested routines start paused.
