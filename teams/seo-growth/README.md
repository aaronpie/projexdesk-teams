# SEO Growth Team

A four-person search team that moves from evidence to an actionable roadmap.

## Members

- **Nova — SEO Strategist:** prioritizes the roadmap and coordinates the team.
- **Scout — Keyword Researcher:** maps intent, clusters, gaps, and opportunities.
- **Atlas — Technical SEO Auditor:** diagnoses crawl, index, rendering, and performance problems.
- **Quill — SEO Content Editor:** turns validated opportunities into useful briefs and content.

## Included skills

- **Keyword Opportunity Map:** converts query evidence into prioritized clusters.
- **Technical SEO Triage:** produces reproducible, severity-ranked findings.
- **Content Brief:** creates a useful brief without keyword stuffing.

## Helpful connections

Google Search Console, Google Analytics, and Google Sheets are useful but not required. Connect accounts inside OpenMausBot; never put credentials in a team file.

## Try it

> Review the search data I provide, identify our three strongest growth opportunities, and build a 30-day plan with owners and success measures.
