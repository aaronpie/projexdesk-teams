# 100x Marketing Team

A four-person growth team built to ship focused campaigns and learn quickly.

## Members

- **Maya — Growth Lead:** chooses the bet, success metric, and priorities.
- **Echo — Creative and Copy Lead:** builds the concept, message, and channel-ready assets.
- **Orbit — Distribution Lead:** designs repeatable, non-spammy distribution.
- **Lens — Growth Analyst:** defines measurement and converts results into the next decision.

## Included skills

- **Positioning Brief:** sharpens the audience, category, promise, and proof.
- **Campaign Sprint:** turns one growth bet into an owned launch plan.
- **Experiment Review:** produces an evidence-based stop, iterate, or scale decision.

## Helpful connections

Google Analytics, Google Sheets, and Slack can help with execution and reporting. Connect accounts inside OpenMausBot; never add credentials to a template.
