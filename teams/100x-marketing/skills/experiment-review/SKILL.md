# Experiment Review

## Use this when

A campaign or growth test has reached its decision date or produced enough evidence for a review.

## Process

1. Restate the original hypothesis, primary metric, baseline, threshold, audience, and time window.
2. Check tracking quality, sample composition, external changes, and missing data before interpreting results.
3. Compare the primary outcome with the threshold and use supporting metrics to diagnose why.
4. Separate observations from explanations and state the confidence of each explanation.
5. Decide to stop, iterate, scale, or gather more data.
6. Define the next action, owner, cost cap, and what would change the decision.

## Output

Include decision, evidence, data-quality notes, interpretation, confidence, lessons, and the next experiment or rollout step.

## Guardrails

Do not cherry-pick supporting metrics, hide negative outcomes, or imply causation from correlation. Avoid false precision when sample size or tracking is weak.
